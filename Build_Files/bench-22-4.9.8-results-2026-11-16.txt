=== THE FIELD EPISODE ON 4.9.8 (app log 2026-11-16T07-04-45, BEFORE this bench) — what it proves ===
The user's replug roulette hit the 2-beep state ON 4.9.8 and it behaved exactly as on 4.9.7:

Wire truth from that log (HB/BH lines, fix E's sns encoding working):
  - sns=0000 (all four missing) and sns=0111 (joystick-only missing) alternate across boots,
    including a REGRESSION 0111 -> 0000 across one plain SR (07:01:14 -> 07:01:42);

  - NO "INFO: I2C scan:" heal note ever fired — the 3-pass retry RAN on every bad boot and ALL
    THREE passes NACKed. The scan retry + boot bus-clear cannot heal this state;
  - the final healthy boot reported CM=1 (the recovery attempts' mode changes), so its BT,0
    refusal was by-design (USB-mode boot), not a BLE failure.
REVISED DIAGNOSIS (supersedes the "stranded protocol state" reading in CHANGES 4.9.8):
  The 2-beep state is ELECTRICAL, not protocol: the master is electrically ALONE on the bus.
  A floating-but-pulled-up master bus reads exactly like a NACK on every probe (no timeout, no
  recovery — matching recov=0/bus=1), so the state is "slaves unpowered or the bus wires between
  master and slaves are open". The staged, non-monotonic healing (0000 -> 0111 -> 0000 -> healthy
  across replugs/handling) is contact wobble, not state-machine realignment; the user's "1 beep +
  restart heals" observation fits a 1-beep boot's HEAVY bus traffic (display init + framebuffer
  pushes + sensor reads) re-making a marginal contact, while a 2-beep safe boot generates no I2C
  traffic at all and heals nothing. 4.9.8's A-D fixes remain correct and necessary (they close the
  firmware-side strand classes: mid-transaction resets — proven by B22's 8/8 — the app-side
  floating window, slow slave power-up; fix E's sns lines carried the diagnosis in the field log),
  but they cannot heal an open circuit, and the ~1.5 s BOOTLOADER floating window (before the app
  runs, on every boot incl. warm resets) remains unreachable from the app.

=== ADDENDUM (2026-11-16 ~11:00): THE 4.9.9 VDD MATRIX — THE RAIL IS EXONERATED ===
10 more replug cycles on 4.9.9 (BH field 29 + HB vdd= live): 7/10 two-beep boots, 3/10 healthy —
and VDD AT THE CHIP WAS NOMINAL ON EVERY BOOT, dead and healthy alike: 3322-3339 mV, no sag, no
difference between states (field-29 read at probe time + the HB line's vdd= on the stuck boots:
3323-3339 mV). The USB cable, the regulator, and the +3V3 path at the module are EXONERATED; the
dead state is NOT a rail sag the MCU can see.
CONSEQUENCE: with the rail nominal at the module and BOTH connectors' devices (J1 display + J2 pod)
vanishing together, the only shared failure element left is the XIAO module's own connection to the
hub board — the socketed module's header contacts for SDA/SCL (or 3V, whose joint breaks the slaves'
supply while the module runs from VBUS). Master-alone-on-the-bus is exactly what a lifted SDA/SCL
header joint produces: the chip's internal pull-ups hold the lines, every probe reads a clean NACK,
recov=0 bus=1. The stochasticity maps to board/enclosure flex during each replug; warm resets can
never heal it (no mechanical change); the user's "slow replug heals" observation = different seating.
The single-device 1-beep shapes (joystick-only, mouthpiece-only, across the field logs) remain a
second, milder thread — pod-side wiring or per-device marginality — not the shared joint.
NEXT PHYSICAL STEPS (decisive, 2 minutes): during a 2-beep boot press/reseat the XIAO module and
watch for the devices popping back; then pull-inspect-reseat the module (dull pins / spread socket
contacts); optional multimeter at J1.2 during a dead boot (3.3 V = SDA/SCL joint, 0 V = 3V joint).

=== ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED (2026-11-16 ~11:15): THE XIAO SOCKET JOINT ===
User's physical test during a 2-beep boot: pressing/"messing with" the XIAO module restored ALL
FOUR devices immediately. The all-devices-missing boot state is an intermittent connection between
the socketed XIAO module and the hub board (SDA/SCL or 3V header contacts) — handling during each
replug flexes the board/module, warm resets can never heal it (no mechanical change), and the
rail self-measurement (4.9.9 BH field 29 / HB vdd=) is what localized it to the module boundary
from the wire alone. REMEDIATION (maker-level): pull the module, inspect pins + socket contacts
(dull/oxidized pins, spread sockets), clean (isopropyl), reseat firmly; if it recurs, reflow or
replace the female headers. The single-device 1-beep shapes remain a milder second thread (pod
wiring/per-device marginality). FIRMWARE CONTRIBUTIONS STAND: 4.9.8 closed the reset-manufactured
strand class (B22 8/8); 4.9.9's vdd field turned a multimeter question into a wire answer.
